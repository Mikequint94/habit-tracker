68-Point Scoring Formula

Your daily grid has 8 daily habits × 7 days = 56 points, and your 12 weekly goals = 12 points (1 point each) for a total of 68 points.

Choose how you want the 12 weekly points calculated. This applies to in-progress weeks; finalized weeks keep the formula they were finalized with.
